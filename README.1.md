**RetailMart – Retail Sales \& Customer Analytics**Project OverviewRetailMart is a retail sales analysis project developed to understand customer behaviour, product performance, sales trends, and business revenue.The project uses SQL to analyse retail transaction data and generate meaningful business insights.**Project Objectives**\- Calculate total revenue.\- Identify best-selling products.\- Analyse city-wise revenue.\- Identify repeat customers.\- Analyse monthly sales performance.\- Evaluate product category performance.**Tools \& Technologies**\- MySQL\- SQL\- MySQL Workbench**Database Tables**1\. Customers2\. Products3\. Orders4\. Order\_Items**Dataset Details**\- Customers: 100 records\- Products: 30 records\- Orders: 300 records\- Order Items: 500 records**SQL Analysis**The project includes 15 business questions covering:\- Aggregate functions\- Filtering and sorting\- Joins\- Group By and Having\- Subqueries\- Common Table Expressions (CTEs)\- Date functions**Project Goal**To transform retail transaction data into useful business insights that support data-driven decision-making.
